Space Unfold
Apartment Interiors

Location : Ernakulam
Year : 2021
Status : Completed

Spaces made by builders for Apartment living, even in premium projects often end up cramped, as an undue response to economy driven planning. This instance, in a premium apartment complex unfortunately had only 11’(330cm) wide grids, boxed up as living spaces.

Interior design solution often is expected to enliven the given mundane canvas with, surfaces, aspiration & life - but beyond that, were there other opportunities? How does one modulate space, as a continuous unfold,(-as against one large open space)-while breaking walls? Can surfaces reinforce notions of domains of living? How does one maintain intimacy?

The 2000sft, 3 bedroom apartment had living room, dining room and kitchen planned with a dimensions of 330 x 660 x 270 cm (wxdxh) including circulation space , which ended up having a very narrow aspect ratio. Breaking down walls as obvious as it seemed, were done with these principles in mind.

Opening up, and Shifting axes Inorder to create a notion of larger space, we opened up the dining- kitchen wall, and the kitchen uitility area walls. As one enters the dark foyer, the light from the balcony sets the initial linear axis. Only at the end of living one encounters a perpendicular axis, facing the dining wall, which quickly shifts laterally along the length of the dining cum island counter to the heart of the kitchen. The Kitchen- Utility space connect helps guide another cross axis along the core of the private kitchen. These shifting axes aid in a continuous unfold of spaces, creating engagement and discovery.

Domains of living /Intimacy At the end of every common space, is a cosy intimate haven. At the end of living room, is a pink low casual seating, contrasting with grey walls walls around, shielded behind a warm teakwood partition. The dining table ends in a barbeque grill top for casual meet ups. The open kitchen ends in a shelf screened utility space. And the dining wall panelling hides the hand wash space. Natural Teak wood surfaces adds warmth to space.

The individual bedrooms were done as per individual tastes of the users, color themes and preferences. Recurring themes of vertical lines in surface textures, color accents against neutral backdrops were followed through.

Surfaces The dining panelling (neutral colour marble finish), and living wall panelling (pastel colored painted); both serve to demarcate respective functions within the space unfold. The vertical lines of these wall panellings tend to make the space feel taller. The dining panelling also extends over to hide the wash counter behind it.

Similarly on the ground plane, darker oak wooden flooring serves to bind spaces for food. The surface of countertops were realised as textureless/ grainless , so that it does not stand out against the darker binding floor beneath.

The project tries to see living space a continuous yet intimate sequence of unfold, orchestrated through movement and tempered with surfaces.
