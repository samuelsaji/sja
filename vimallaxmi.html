Vimal-Laxmi Residence

Location :Kochi
Year : 2020
Status : Completed

The house is set in an old & familiar neigh- borhood where the lady of the house grew up in.

Familiarity of context, shared memories, and a flowering Kanikonna tree(cassia fistula ); formed the canvas that informed the design decisions one was to take.

While catering to the typical program of a 3 bedroom house, it was essential that the architecture of the house, and its afterlife would maintain, & enliven the cordiality that the neighbourhood had been offering since generations..

This conversation essentially started at the compound wall which relieves itself from its purpose of ‘guarding’, to ‘accommodating’ – a space with a thickness

Such a gesture of the house creates a common space for the neighbourhood, used for friendly evening chats, playtime banter, resting after evening walks...

This social engagement is connected to the house through the stepped verandah, which has moments of pause through subtle modulation of the plinth & seats that catch the western breeze.

The main door at the end of the verandah is recessed deep; shaded and cool; along which one slides into the core of the house.

The core of the house developed on a transverse east west section - catching the breeze & the changing light, and a courtyard that connects the house together. The courtyard is where pookalams are put for Onam festival,but also where casual cross legged chats happen on built in platforms.

Walls that filter light and breeze, and the Kanikonna foliage make an interesting ever changing chiaroscuro that defines the spatiality of the house.

Motifs inspired from the kanikonna, finds place in floor patterns, jalis & carvings.

The ground floor has (apart from the living spaces) the kitchen, a bedroom and a family den. The family den in the ground floor accommodates all their superstars- figurines, cds ,books of Tintin, Avengers etc to a custom made(Andy Warhol pop inspired) painting of all their Malayalam movie superstars, by artist Hariprasad.

As one walks up the stair, scale gradually expands through varying volumes.The first floor accommodates bedrooms, a study, a laundry room and a terrace under the foliage of the tree.

The house is a space that accommodates- activities, identities & quirkiness of all characters, while being rooted to fundamentals of context & climate and socially inclusive of the way it tries to engage with its surrounds.
